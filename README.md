# Open Data Infrastructure for Social Impact 

** Executive Summary and Strategic Leadership Impact**
Data infrastructure for social impact is an end-to-end data pipeline and interactive analytics ecosystem designed to evaluate and optimize early-years community engagement sessions. Prior to this deployment, community organisers relied on paper based disjointed weekly logs, making resource scaling and funding allocation inefficient. 
As the sole Data Science Lead on this initiative, I developed grant ready impact report to enable community event organisers measure social impact and scale.

 

### Operational Intelligence Architecture
To move the organisation from fragmented paper based logging to a data-driven paradigm, I architected and deployed a centralized data pipeline and analytics dashboard. This dashboard functions as a critical business intelligence tool, providing local government authorities and institutional donors with verifiable and audited impact metrics.




### Result

1.	Data-driven funding acquisition: translated qualitative operational metrics into hard, visual statistics, providing the quantitative proof-of-impact required to secure local authority funding and donors.
2.	Resource optimisation: uncovered exact correlations between child age brackets and programmatic needs, leading to a 74% precision rate in meal provision management and targeted toy inventory budgeting.
